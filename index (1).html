Overview

Employee Central Payroll (ECP) is SAP’s cloud payroll solution. It takes employee, pay and time data from Employee Central, runs gross-to-net payroll, and posts results to Finance, banks and statutory bodies.

Approach

  • SAP Activate fit-to-standard method
  • Iterative configuration with regular demos
  • PMI-style governance and formal gates

Scope

  • India statutory payroll: PF, ESI, PT, TDS, Form 16, Form 24Q
  • EC to ECP replication, Finance posting, bank files
  • Testing, training, cutover, hypercare

Key assumptions

  • Single big-bang go-live
  • Two parallel payroll runs before go-live
  • Legacy payroll kept until the third live cycle is accepted

Implementation phases

Six SAP Activate phases, each with an exit gate.

1. Discover

Month 1
  • Confirm scope and business case
  • Check EC readiness

2. Prepare

Months 1 to 2
  • Charter, plan, RAID log
  • Environments and data profiling

3. Explore

Months 2 to 3
  • Fit-to-standard workshops
  • Gap register and test strategy

4. Realize

Months 3 to 10
  • Configuration, interfaces, data mocks
  • SIT, UAT, two parallel runs

5. Deploy

Months 10 to 11
  • Dress rehearsal and final load
  • Go/No-Go and first live payroll

6. Run

Months 11 to 14
  • Three-cycle hypercare
  • Handover and post-implementation review

Contingency plan

Payroll cannot be deferred, so a tested fallback stays available until hypercare ends.

Go/No-Go criteria

  • No open critical or high defects
  • Two parallel runs reconciled, 99.5 percent net-pay match
  • Bank, Finance and statutory outputs tested
  • 90 percent of users trained
  • Dress rehearsal completed

Scenarios

  • Go-live postponed: move to next payroll period
  • Errors before payment: hold file, fix, or run legacy that cycle
  • Errors after payment: off-cycle correction within 24 hours
  • Interface outage: manual file procedure

Rollback principles

  • Legacy stays available until the third live cycle
  • Steering Committee decides before bank cut-off
  • Every cutover step has an owner and check
